SUBSCRIPTION MANAGEMENT

Stop paying for apps you forgot existed.

SubZero finds forgotten subscriptions, catches unexpected price increases, and helps you cancel unwanted charges—without the usual support-call headache.

Find my hidden subscriptions

No credit card required to scan.

Subscribing takes 5 seconds. Canceling takes 15 minutes of digging through hidden settings.

Software companies know that if they make cancellation annoying enough, you’ll procrastinate on doing it. A few dollars here and there doesn't feel urgent—until you add up six months of quiet monthly charges for tools you haven't opened once.

The "I’ll Just Try It" Free Trial

You signed up for a 7-day trial to finish one project, forgot to set a reminder, and ended up paying for the entire year.

The Stealth Price Hike

That $9/month tool quietly bumped its price to $14/month. No heads-up, no email notification—just a larger charge hitting your statement every 30 days.

The Cancellation Dark Pattern

Hidden buttons, multi-page exit surveys, and mandatory phone calls are engineered to make you give up before you hit "confirm."

"You don't need a complex budgeting app with 50 pie charts. You just need a tool that flags the leaks and helps you stop them."

HOW SUBZERO WORKS

From hidden leak to canceled charge in under two minutes.

No spreadsheets. No manually typing in transaction names. SubZero automates the detection so you only have to make one decision: keep it or kill it.

01

Connect in 30 Seconds

Link your bank feed or upload a statement using read-only, bank-level 256-bit encryption. SubZero never sees your credentials or touches your funds.

02

Spot Every Silent Leak

Our scanner instantly isolates recurring charges, groups them by service, and highlights stealth price hikes and ending free trials you might have missed.

03

Cancel Without the Headaches

Select any unwanted subscription and hit "Cancel." SubZero routes through automated cancellation channels or gives you direct, single-click bypass links to skip customer support traps.

100% read-only access. SubZero cannot move money, make purchases, or alter your bank account.

# SubZero — B2C SaaS Copywriting & UX Case Study

A high-converting, minimalist spec landing page, lifecycle onboarding email, and product UX microcopy showcase built for **SubZero**, a consumer subscription management app.

This project demonstrates an end-to-end B2C copywriting workflow—from high-level positioning and pain-led landing page copy down to re-engagement emails and in-app retention modals.

---

## 🎯 The Copywriting Strategy

### Core Positioning: Direct & Pain-Led
Rather than leading with vague promises ("Reclaim your financial freedom") or complex feature lists, SubZero focuses on the immediate, relatable friction point: **paying for things you forgot you signed up for.**

* **Headline:** *"Stop paying for apps you forgot existed."*
* **Core Pain Points Targeted:**
  1. **Forgotten Free Trials:** The classic 7-day trial that turns into a full annual charge.
  2. **Stealth Price Hikes:** Subtle $2–$5 monthly rate increases that pass unnoticed on bank statements.
  3. **Cancellation Dark Patterns:** Intentional user-experience hurdles (hidden buttons, phone call requirements, exit surveys) designed to stop cancellations.

---

## 📦 What’s Inside (Key Deliverables)

### 1. High-Converting Landing Page (`index.html`)
* **Sticky Glassmorphic Nav & Portfolio Banner:** Seamless navigation with transparent attribution as a spec project.
* **Hero Section:** Direct headline, friction-reducing subcopy, clear CTA (*"Find my hidden subscriptions"*), and risk reversal.
* **Agitation Section:** A 3-column micro-pain grid shifting blame off the consumer and onto predatory subscription tactics.
* **How It Works:** A 3-step process proving that fixing the leak requires zero manual entry or spreadsheet tracking.
* **Objection Handling (FAQ):** Addresses 256-bit encryption, read-only permissions, and safety before the final CTA.

### 2. Lifecycle Email (Onboarding / Re-engagement)
* **Trigger:** Automated "First Scan Complete" notification.
* **Subject Line:** *We found $47/month in hidden charges on your account.*
* **Strategy:** Instantly quantifies the annual financial leak ($500+) and offers a single-click resolution path.

### 3. In-App UX Microcopy
* **Cancellation Confirmation Modal:** Clear, transparent language that sets expectations on remaining billing cycle access.
* **Success State:** Instant positive reinforcement displaying exact dollars saved for the year.
